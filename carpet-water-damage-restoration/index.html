Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Bellmore, NY

A soaked carpet is two problems stacked together, the carpet on top and the pad underneath. We check both, because the pad is the part that holds water and the part that decides whether the carpet can stay.

Carpet water damage is the wetting of carpet, pad and the subfloor under them. Carpet fibers dry fast, but the foam pad holds water like a sponge, and the tack strips and subfloor beneath it can stay wet long after the surface feels dry.

If your carpet is wet now, stop the source, blot standing water and call our 24/7 line. Keep people off the wet area, lift furniture onto foil or blocks and do not use a household vacuum on standing water.

Carpet, pad and subfloor: what each one holds

Carpet is a surface layer. The pad is where water goes, and the subfloor is where it can stay. A technician can lift a corner, check the pad and probe the subfloor to see how far the water spread. Without that check, a carpet that feels dry on top can hide a wet pad for days.

When the carpet pad should come out

Pad often has to go even when the carpet stays. Pad soaks up water, dries slowly and cannot be cleaned inside. If the water was clean and the wetting was caught within a day or so, pad can sometimes be dried in place. After that, or if the water was dirty, it is usually removed.

Replacing a pad is far cheaper than a mold problem under the carpet, and the carpet itself can often be cleaned, dried and put back on new pad.

Wet carpet and mold timing

Mold needs moisture, and a damp pad under a sealed carpet is a good place for it. The practical window is the first couple of days, because drying that starts later has to work against damp materials that have been wet longer.

If wet carpet has sat for several days, mold remediation may be a separate question after drying. Testing and remediation follow ANSI/IICRC S520, a published standard, and health questions belong with a physician.

How we dry carpet in place

Extraction removes water first, often with a weighted tool that pulls from both sides. Air movers then push air under a lifted carpet corner or through a floating section, while dehumidifiers pull the moisture out of the room.

Readings are taken at each visit, and equipment stays until the carpet, pad and subfloor all read normal. The structural drying page explains the equipment and moisture mapping in more detail.

First steps when carpet is wet

Before the crew arrives

  • Stop the source and remove standing water with towels or a wet vacuum made for water.
  • Lift furniture off the carpet and put foil or plastic under the legs.
  • Remove rugs and loose items, and hang wet items to dry.
  • Open windows only if the weather allows, and run air conditioning to keep humidity down.
  • Photograph the wet area and any stains before cleanup.

Stairs, closets and tack strips

Carpet on stairs and in closets is easy to overlook. Water runs to the lowest point, so a closet floor next to a wet room can hold more than the room itself.

Tack strips are wooden and absorb water along the edges of a room. They are checked and replaced if they stay wet, because a damp strip can wick moisture into new carpet.

Carpet after Bellmore storms and flooding

Local press reported several feet of water in parts of South Bellmore during Hurricane Sandy in 2012. Carpet that sat in water from a surge like that is almost always removed, because the water is contaminated and the pad cannot be cleaned. If your carpet is wet from a leak, hardwood nearby may be affected too, which is covered under hardwood floor water damage.
