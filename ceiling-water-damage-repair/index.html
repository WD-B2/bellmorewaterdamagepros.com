Emergency Ceiling Water Damage Repair in Bellmore, NY

A wet ceiling means water is above it, and what you see is only part of the story. Our 24/7 line is open for Bellmore ceiling leaks, from a brown stain to a ceiling that has let go.

Ceiling water damage repair is the work of finding where water is coming from, drying the cavity above and below the ceiling, and repairing or replacing the drywall or plaster that was damaged. If a ceiling in your Bellmore home is dripping, bulging or sagging, move people out from under it, shut off the water if a pipe is the source and call our 24/7 line.

A ceiling can hold a surprising weight of water. Do not stand under a bulge, do not poke it unless a crew has told you to, and keep power off to any light fixture the water is touching, but only if the panel is dry and reachable. Call 911 or your utility first for any electrical risk. Then reach dispatch for same-day response when available.

When a wet ceiling is an emergency

A sagging or bulging ceiling is an emergency because it can collapse without warning. Drywall that is soaked loses strength, and insulation above it can add weight. Clear the room, keep children and pets out and call the line.

We do not recommend relieving a bulge yourself unless a crew has told you how. Opening a controlled drain point in the right spot with a bucket below is done on site by trained crews, after the area is checked for wiring and the source is found.

Finding the source above the ceiling

Ceiling water has a limited list of sources, and each one calls for a different fix. An upstairs bathroom supply line or drain, a leaking pipe in the joist space, an HVAC line in the attic and the roof above all show up as the same brown stain below.

A moisture meter and thermal camera trace the wet path across the ceiling and into the framing. If the source is a pipe, the water is shut off and burst pipe water damage steps apply. If the source is the air handler, see AC leak water damage. If it is the roof, the next section applies.

Roof leaks: roof water damage and what comes through the ceiling

Roof water damage often starts small and shows up late. Water enters at a failed shingle, flashing, vent or valley, runs along the decking and rafters and soaks the insulation, and the ceiling stain appears feet from the actual entry point.

Roof leak water damage is common after wind-driven rain, and in Bellmore reports from 2014 described about five inches of rain in six hours. A single storm like that tests every roof in the area, so check ceilings after heavy rain, not only when you see a stain.

Water damage from a leaking roof needs two fixes in the right order. The roof has to be sealed by a roofer first, and we dry the insulation, framing and ceiling below. A temporary tarp over the opening can stop more water while the repair is scheduled.

When a ceiling has collapsed

A collapsed ceiling is a messy, hazardous scene. Wet drywall, insulation and sometimes fixture parts land on the floor, and the open cavity can keep dripping. Stay out of the room until the water is controlled, and do not touch exposed wiring.

The crew removes the debris, extracts the water, opens the cavity enough to dry the framing, and then the ceiling is rebuilt. Insulation that got soaked is usually removed. Any floor under the collapse is treated as a water loss too, and hardwood floor water damage repair covers what to expect if wood flooring was hit.
