What to say when you call

Have these details ready if you can

  • Your address and the nearest cross street
  • Where the water is coming from, if you know, and whether it is still running
  • Which rooms or floors are wet, and for how long
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or dirty, and whether it came from a drain or from outside
  • Whether the electrical panel or any outlets are near the water

While you wait

Shut off the main water valve if a pipe is the cause. Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water. If water is near outlets or a panel, or you smell gas, leave and call 911 or your utility first.

Photograph the damage before anything is moved. Your insurer will likely ask for photos, and moisture readings from the crew add to them. We explain scope and price before work starts, and same-day response is offered when available.
