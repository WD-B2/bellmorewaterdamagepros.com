Emergency Frozen Pipe Water Damage in Bellmore, NY

Frozen pipe water damage is the flooding and wet structure that follows when water freezes inside a supply line, splits the pipe and then runs when the ice melts. If a pipe has frozen or has just started leaking in Bellmore, close the main shutoff, open the lowest faucet to drain the line and call our 24/7 line before the thaw spreads the damage.

Do not use a flame, a torch or a heat gun on a frozen pipe, and do not walk into a room where water is near outlets or the panel. Cut power only if the panel is dry and reachable, and call 911 or your utility first for any electrical or gas risk. Then reach dispatch, where same-day response is offered when available.

Why a frozen pipe floods when it thaws

Water expands as it freezes. The ice plug itself does not usually burst the pipe. Pressure builds in the trapped water between the plug and a closed faucet, and the weakest point of that section gives way, often a few feet from the ice.

The split can sit unnoticed while the pipe is frozen, because the ice is still acting as a plug. When the temperature rises or the heat comes back on, the plug melts and the pipe runs at full household pressure through the split.

What not to do

Never use an open flame, a propane torch or a heat gun on a pipe. They can ignite framing and can crack a weakened pipe. Do not leave a faucet closed on a pipe you think is split, and do not cut power if you must stand in water to reach the panel.

Where pipes freeze inside a house

Exterior walls

A pipe on the cold side of insulation can chill faster than the room around it.

Crawl spaces and garages

Unheated spaces with exposed supply lines give ice room to form. See crawl space water damage for what happens under the floor.

Attics and cabinets

Lines near roof decking or on an outside wall behind a sink cabinet get little warmth.

Rooms left cold

A vacant room or a shut-off wing can fall low enough to freeze a line even in a heated house.

After the thaw: what the damage usually looks like

A split behind a wall wets insulation and drywall first, then runs down to the bottom plate and into the subfloor. A split above a ceiling stains and sags the ceiling below, and water can travel along joists to rooms that seem unrelated.

We follow that path with a moisture meter and a thermal camera, pull standing water, and dry the structure with air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. A stained or bowed ceiling is its own repair, and ceiling water damage repair explains what that involves.

Bellmore homes, the supplier on your bill and permits

Your water bill names your supplier, which matters if you cannot find the main valve or the curb stop. The Town of Hempstead Water Department does not list Bellmore among its districts, so the bill is the reliable place to look.

If a section of pipe has to be replaced, ask the Town of Hempstead Building Department whether a permit applies, since permit rules vary and the department enforces plumbing codes.
