Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this website or call our line.

Effective November 9, 2026. Bellmore Water Damage Pros, 2410 Merrick Road, Bellmore, NY 11710, operates this website and the phone line (516) 845-9582.

Calls to our line

This site has no forms. We take requests by phone. When you call, we ask for your name, the property address, a callback number and a description of the damage so that we can send a crew.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the person who answers and they will explain your options.

What the website collects

Like most websites, ours may record basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location. This information helps us keep the site working and understand which pages are useful.

Our pages embed Google Maps and load fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may collect information under their own policies when a page loads.

How information is used

We use call information to dispatch crews, to explain scope and price, and to follow up on the work. We use website information to maintain and improve the site. We do not use call recordings for any purpose other than quality, training and handling your request.

Sharing

To perform the work you request, we share the details needed with the restoration professionals sent to your address. We share information where the law requires it. We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to your request.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about a call, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Call (516) 845-9582 or email contact@bellmorewaterdamagepros.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
