Water heater leak cleanup is the removal of water and the drying of floors, walls and stored items after a tank, valve or connection fails. If a water heater is leaking in your Bellmore home right now, shut off the cold water supply to the tank, turn off the power or gas as described below, and call our 24/7 line.

For an electric heater, switch off its breaker only if the panel is dry and reachable. For a gas heater, turn the control to off, and if you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your utility before you call us. Keep out of water that touches the unit or its wiring. Then call dispatch, where same-day response is offered when available.

What fails on a water heater

The tank itself

Metal tanks corrode from the inside over time. When the lining fails, water seeps or pours from the base.

The relief valve

This safety valve opens when pressure or temperature rises and may drip if it wears out.

The drain valve

A small valve near the base can weep and soak the floor slowly.

The supply connections

Flexible lines and fittings at the top can loosen or corrode.

Where the water goes after a tank fails

Water heaters sit in utility rooms, closets, garages and basements, so the water often reaches stored boxes, drywall bottoms and floor coverings before anyone notices. A slow leak behind the unit can wet the wall bottom for weeks.

We extract standing water, check the drywall and baseboard with a moisture meter and dry the area with air movers and dehumidifiers. If the heater is in a lower level, the cleanup may overlap with basement water damage cleanup. If the water spread across a kitchen or laundry room floor, appliance leak water damage covers a related cleanup.

Drain pans, pressure and why a replacement matters

A drain pan under the tank with a pipe to a drain catches small leaks, and many installations skip it. If your heater has no pan, a leak goes straight to the floor. Ask your plumber about a pan and a leak alarm when the unit is replaced.

If the tank is the source, it will need replacing by a licensed plumber. We dry and restore the water damage, and the two jobs can be scheduled so the drying equipment is not in the plumber's way.

Bellmore, permits and your water supplier

Your water bill names your supplier, and the Town of Hempstead Water Department does not list Bellmore among its districts, so the bill is the place to check if you cannot find the main shutoff. A plumber replacing a tank may need a permit, and permit rules vary, so ask the Town of Hempstead Building Department.
